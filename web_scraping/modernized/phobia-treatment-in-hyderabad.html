Understanding Phobias

A phobia is an intense, irrational fear of a specific object, situation, or activity. The fear is disproportionate to the actual danger and can significantly impact daily life and functioning.

Common Types

  • Agoraphobia (fear of open spaces)
  • Social phobia (social anxiety)
  • Specific phobias (heights, animals, etc.)
  • Claustrophobia (fear of enclosed spaces)
  • Acrophobia (fear of heights)

Treatment Approach

We offer exposure therapy, cognitive-behavioral therapy, relaxation techniques, and medication management to help individuals overcome phobias and regain control of their lives.
