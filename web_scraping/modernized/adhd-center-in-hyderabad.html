Understanding ADHD

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) is a neurodevelopmental disorder that affects children and can continue into adulthood. It's characterized by difficulties with attention, hyperactivity, and impulsivity.

Common Symptoms

  • Difficulty paying attention
  • Excessive activity or restlessness
  • Impulsive behavior
  • Difficulty following instructions
  • Frequently forgetting things

Treatment Approach

Our team uses a multidisciplinary approach including behavioral therapy, medication management, and parent training to help children with ADHD thrive academically and socially.
