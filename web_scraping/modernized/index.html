Specialities

We offer comprehensive treatments & therapies for a vast range of Neuropsychiatric Conditions under one roof.

Anger Management

Outbursts of anger are not only harmful for your near and dear ones, but it can also affect your reputation in the society.

Stress Management

Stress is a strain on your psychological and emotional wellbeing. If not managed properly, it can reduce your productivity.

Anxiety Management

Anxiety or a sudden experience of all-consuming fear can impact your social, professional and personal lives.

Deaddiction Treatment

Addiction is a self-destructive habit that consumes your individuality and steals a normal life away from you.

Counselling

Counseling is a crucial treatment procedure in psychiatric healing. It is used to complement behavior-modifying medicines.

Depression

Depression is a feeling of engulfing sadness or 'doom' that makes you lose the motivation to lead a normal life.

Marital Counselling

When two people live in the same space, it can definitely lead to major disagreements at times. Marriage counseling can help you.

Sleep Disorders

Insomnia or hypersomnia can affect the normal functioning of your body and mind; it can also be a symptom of a more serious medical condition.
