Understanding Bipolar Disorder

Bipolar disorder is a mental health condition characterized by extreme mood swings that include emotional highs (mania or hypomania) and lows (depression). These mood swings can affect sleep, energy, activity, judgment, behavior, and the ability to think clearly.

Common Symptoms

  • Extreme mood swings
  • Decreased need for sleep (mania)
  • Racing thoughts
  • Impulsive behavior
  • Feelings of sadness (depression)

Treatment Approach

Bipolar disorder requires lifelong treatment with mood stabilizers, psychotherapy, and lifestyle management. Our team provides comprehensive care to help manage symptoms and improve quality of life.
